# 🚀 Zen Launcher

**A clean, fast, and customizable Android experience.**

Smart Launch turns your Android phone into a faster, cleaner, and more organized companion.

---

## ✨ Why You’ll Love Smart Launch

- 🎨 **Make It Yours**: Easily customize colors, themes, and layouts to match your style.
- 📁 **Smart Folder Organization**: Group your favorite apps together so your home screen stays neat and uncluttered.
- 🔍 **Instant Search**: Find any application on your phone in a single tap.
- ⚡ **Lightweight & Fast**: Enjoy smooth animations and lightning-fast navigation without slowing down your phone.
- 🛡️ **Privacy First**: No ads, no tracking, and zero hidden background services.

## 📥 How to Download & Install

1. Go to the **[Releases Section](../../releases)** of this repository.
2. Download the latest `.apk` file to your Android phone.
3. Open the downloaded file and tap **Install** (if prompted, allow installation from your browser).
4. Press your phone's **Home** button, select **Smart Launch**, and set it as your **Always** default launcher!

## 🙋 Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Will this drain my battery?</b></summary>
<br>
No! Smart Launch is designed from the ground up to be ultra-lightweight and battery-efficient.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I switch back to my default home screen?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes, at any time. Just go to your phone’s <i>Settings → Apps → Default Apps → Home app</i> and pick your original launcher.
</details>
